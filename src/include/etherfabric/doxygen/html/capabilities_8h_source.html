24 /*
25  * List of capabilities that can be queried.
26  *
27  * These describe the theoretical availability of a resource in the
28  * hardware's current configuration, rather than the current
29  * availability of that resource, or if the hardware would be capable
30  * in a different configuration. However, for those capabilities where
31  * permissions are relevant, the reported value does reflects whether
32  * the caller is permitted to access that resource, either due to
33  * licences, NIC configuration, or user privileges
